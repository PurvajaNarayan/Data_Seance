1. Human oversight  
   - Design data pipelines and models so humans can inspect inputs, labels, features, and key decisions; enable override, rollback, and review of model changes.

2. Technical robustness and safety  
   - Ensure data quality checks, robust preprocessing, reproducible training, stress tests, and monitoring for drift and failure modes.

3. Privacy and data governance  
   - Collect and use data lawfully with minimization, anonymization/pseudonymization where appropriate, access control, and integrity checks across the lifecycle.

4. Transparency  
   - Document data sources, selection criteria, feature definitions, labeling processes, model architectures, training procedures, and versions; ensure traceability from outputs back to data and model artifacts.

5. Diversity, non-discrimination and fairness  
   - Identify protected attributes and proxies; perform subgroup performance and bias analyses; avoid unjustified disparate impact and document mitigation steps.

6. Accountability  
   - Maintain audit logs, documentation, and version control for datasets, code, models, and experiments to support internal and external review.
